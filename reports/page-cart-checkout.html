Evidence

Cart heatmap — captured for the first time, 4 Aug

Every prior attempt at a cart heatmap failed outright ("no supplied click texts could be located") because a fresh page load always lands on an empty cart, and the highest-weight clicks (the login modal, the new coupon module, "Proceed to Checkout") only exist once an item is in cart — a state a stateless screenshot can't reproduce. This pass got a partial result: two of the cart page's real click targets — the Cart1/3 step indicator and the empty-cart messaging — do exist on the empty state and rendered successfully below. The login-modal and coupon-module clicks (the majority of the table above) still can't be captured this way; checkout-gate.js's screenshots (further down this page) remain the only way to see those, since that script actually adds an item first.

Read carefully: this doesn't mean users are mostly clicking the step indicator instead of shopping — it means a meaningful share of cart-page sessions in Clarity's aggregate are landing on an empty cart (direct visit, bookmark, or after removing the last item), separate from the cart-with-item sessions the rest of this page's findings are about.

What the live view above shows

This is the most qualified traffic on the entire site. Across every range in the filter above, engagement rate runs 90%+ on both devices with a near-zero new-user share — confirming cart visitors are committed shoppers who already browsed and added an item, not casual traffic. Every session lost to the OTP wall here is a near-certain buyer, which is why T1/T2 outrank almost everything else in the plan on impact-per-fix.

Recommendations

What to do next, by device

Mobile

  1. Ship T1 (bottom sheet) first — of every fix on the site, this one acts on the most qualified, highest-intent traffic (97% engaged, 1.4% new).
  2. T2 (revised) is the structural fix behind T1's layout change — move the login trigger to "Place Order" commitment and keep cart/total visible throughout, matching CaratLane's flow; sequence with T1 rather than shipping the layout fix alone and stopping.
  3. Ship T14 (fire begin_checkout on the click, not after OTP) alongside T1/T2 — without it, T1's impact will be invisible in GA4.

Desktop

  1. No cart-specific desktop work needed — 92.8% engagement and 144.6s average duration are healthy, and this is the proof that the centred-dialog pattern (vs mobile's full-screen takeover) works.
  2. Use desktop's CART 1/3 progress-visible pattern as the reference design when building T1's mobile bottom sheet.

Both are blocked; only Android fights back visibly. Bounce is near-zero on both OS (3.1% Android, 1.5% iOS) and scroll depth is similarly high (80–83%) — everyone is stuck, not leaving. But the click signature is dramatically different: Android hammers the gate itself ("REQUEST OTP" clicked 1,864 times, the "WELCOME TO LUCIRA" modal 1,153 times, the Cart 1/3 step indicator 794 times) for 968 rage clicks total. iOS's top clicks are almost entirely normal cart actions ("Remove" 85, "Proceed to Checkout" 60, "Apply Coupon" 28) with "REQUEST OTP" appearing only 23 times and zero rage clicks — iOS visitors seem to try alternate paths (coupon, wishlist) rather than repeatedly hammering the blocked button. Same wall, measurably more infuriating on Android.

Deployment check · 4 Aug

Cart, shipping & payment: what changed, what broke, what's fine

GA4 tracking broke again, sitewide, the day after the deployment — and is still live this morning. 2 Aug was completely normal on every checkout page (cart mobile 97.8% engaged, shipping/payment bounce 0%). On 3 Aug — the day immediately after the deployment — every checkout page collapsed identically: cart mobile engaged fell to 1.3% (bounce 98.7%), shipping mobile bounce jumped 0%→76.5%, payment mobile bounce jumped 0%→78.6%, payment desktop bounce 0-30%→87.5%.

Two independent signals prove real behaviour did not change: funnel events stayed normal (2 purchases, 96 begin_checkout, 36 add_shipping_info, 19 add_payment_info combined across devices on 3 Aug — in the usual daily range) and Clarity's aggregate numbers for the whole site stayed normal too (11,505 mobile sessions, 544 rage clicks, 26.5% scroll). Live console check this morning reproduced Skipped GTM→GA4: Missing data for: pageView on 3 of 3 reloads of this page — up from 1 of 3 previously. See F12/T22 in Insights and the Action Plan for the full timeline.

Update: this has kept recurring well past the 3 Aug deployment window (also broken on 4, 5, 7 and 9 Aug, with no new release shipped) — now 7 of the last 9 days checked. The deployment-correlation theory has been ruled out; this is being treated as a standing GA4 processing characteristic (self-corrects within 2-3 days) rather than a regression from this specific deployment.
