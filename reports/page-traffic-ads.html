Both errors are specifically the Meta in-app browser failing to bridge native calls: window.webkit.messageHandlers undefined (iOS) and postMessage: Java object is gone (Android). This is what happens when someone clicks an Instagram/Facebook ad and the checkout flow runs inside Meta's own in-app browser instead of Safari/Chrome — a likely contributor to F4's bounce numbers above, and possibly to checkout failures specifically for Meta-sourced traffic (see T17: complete a purchase inside the Instagram/Facebook in-app browser, still open).

Recommendations

What to do next

Mobile — where almost all Meta/Paid Social traffic lands

  1. Complete T17 (finish a purchase inside the Instagram/Facebook in-app browser on a real device) before spending on more Meta traffic — 1,511 sessions already show WebView JS errors, so the checkout may be silently failing for exactly this channel.
  2. Report Paid Social funnel metrics separately from the site blend — its 62–100% bounce and 33% engagement rate will otherwise drag down every aggregate number and hide genuine template performance.

Google Ads — account-level, not device-specific

  1. Pause-and-review the 6 real-spend, zero-conversion campaigns above (₹95,003/30d) — device-level Google Ads breakdowns weren't pulled this round, so treat this as an account-level action for now.
  2. Prioritise the pan-India "checkout" campaign (12,173 clicks, 0 conversions) — investigate via T13 before deciding whether it's a tracking gap or a real mismatch.
