Recommendations

What to do next, by device

Mobile

  1. Ship the interim popup mitigation first (raise NitroCommerce's trigger delay/session-view threshold) — it's the one change that plausibly moves engagement rate, session duration, and the Home→Collection leak all at once.
  2. Track engagement rate (see the filter above) as a secondary success metric alongside the Home→Collection completion rate once that mitigation ships — it should move first and faster.
  3. Don't ship a full redesign here first — the popup is a single, isolated, config-level cause; fix that before considering anything more structural.

Desktop

  1. No homepage-specific desktop work needed — engagement rate and session duration are consistently healthy across every range above, and desktop doesn't show the popup pattern.
  2. Guard rail: when the popup mitigation ships, confirm (via cro_overlay_audit) it doesn't get accidentally applied to desktop in a way that changes this baseline.

These are two different problems, not one "mobile" problem. Android's top clicks are almost entirely the popup/login modal ("Register to Win" 808, "REQUEST OTP" 449, "Spin the Wheel" 308, "WELCOME TO LUCIRA" 287) with 37 rage clicks — Android users get stuck fighting it, which is why engagement/duration still look reasonable (they're actively struggling, not leaving). iOS's top clicks are almost entirely real navigation ("Rings" 122, "Bracelets" 28, "Earrings" 25, "Bestsellers" 24) with zero rage clicks and only one popup click — but bounce is 12 points higher and sessions last half as long. iOS visitors aren't fighting the popup; they appear to be leaving quietly, possibly because it renders differently (or not at all) in iOS Safari/WebView. A single fix for "the popup" won't address both: Android needs the popup suppressed; iOS needs investigating separately (see the WebView JS errors on the Traffic & Meta/Ads page).

Evidence

Live capture, mobile — refreshed 4 Aug

New this pass: the popup is intermittent, not guaranteed on every load. The heatmap capture above and the overlay audit beside it were taken minutes apart, same URL, same mobile profile — the heatmap visit rendered no popup at all, while the overlay-audit visit immediately after got the full 100%-coverage registration modal. Re-running checkout-gate.js and cro_overlay_audit on desktop today both did trigger it (see PDP evidence in the Insights page). This doesn't change the underlying finding — Clarity's aggregate click volume across thousands of real sessions still shows the popup as the dominant click target — but it means a single screenshot is a sample of one, not proof the popup fires every time. Treat "the popup blocked this visit" and "the popup blocks most visits" as two different, both-true claims.
